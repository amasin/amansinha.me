# Product-management brand growth playbook

The website is the owned home for Aman Sinha's professional brand. It should make three things easy to understand: the problems Aman solves, the evidence that he can solve them, and the next useful action for the visitor.

## Positioning

**Category:** Senior product manager and platform product leader

**Distinctive territory:** Enterprise platforms where customer experience, technology, risk, adoption, and economics must work together

**Core message:** Product clarity for systems that cannot afford confusion

**Best-fit opportunities:** Enterprise SaaS, fintech and banking, platform products, AI-powered operations, internal products, and complex customer-experience platforms

## Primary audiences

1. Hiring managers looking for senior or lead Product Managers
2. Product and engineering executives seeking platform leadership
3. Recruiters evaluating scope, outcomes, and role fit quickly
4. PM peers who can discover and share Aman's product thinking

## Content pillars

- Platform product strategy and prioritization
- Internal-product adoption and product operations
- AI automation with controls and human judgment
- Data products, observability, and platform economics
- Enterprise customer experience and transformation

Every article should make one defensible argument, include a practical decision model, and link to a relevant case study or résumé evidence.

## Sustainable publishing cadence

- Publish one strong Product Note every four to six weeks.
- Turn each note into one LinkedIn post, one shorter follow-up, and two thoughtful comments on relevant industry conversations.
- Refresh one case study each quarter with a clearer decision, artifact, learning, or verified outcome.
- Review résumé dates, availability language, metadata, and broken links monthly while actively searching.

Do not publish merely to maintain frequency. A smaller body of useful, original writing builds more trust than generic high-volume content.

## Distribution

- Add `amansinha.me` to the LinkedIn headline/contact section and Feature the strongest case study or Product Note.
- Add the website to the GitHub profile and email signature.
- Link relevant articles naturally in recruiter conversations and interviews.
- Ask trusted former colleagues to share specific public recommendations on LinkedIn; do not copy private praise onto the site without permission.
- Seek a small number of relevant backlinks through guest posts, product communities, alumni networks, and conference or meetup contributions.

## Measurement

Use Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Search Console to monitor:

- Search impressions for Aman's name and target product topics
- Click-through rate for homepage, résumé, case studies, and Product Notes
- Case-study and résumé visits from LinkedIn
- Email and LinkedIn contact actions
- Returning visitors and articles that generate deeper portfolio exploration

Avoid optimizing for raw traffic. The meaningful outcome is qualified discovery and productive product-leadership conversations.

## Post-deployment search checklist

1. Verify the `https://amansinha.me` domain property in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://amansinha.me/sitemap.xml`.
3. Request indexing for the homepage, résumé, three strongest case studies, Insights index, and each Product Note.
4. Confirm the old résumé snippet disappears after recrawl and that no obsolete hosting origin remains indexable.
5. Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools, ideally by importing the Search Console property.
6. Validate the homepage and one article in Google's Rich Results Test.
7. Confirm `www.amansinha.me` redirects permanently to the canonical HTTPS domain.
8. Enable Cloudflare Web Analytics and review real Core Web Vitals after enough traffic is available.
